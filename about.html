Our Journey

The AutoElite Story

A timeline of innovation, excellence, and automotive passion.

2008

The Beginning

AutoElite is founded in Beverly Hills with a single showroom and a vision to revolutionize luxury automotive retail. Our first sale: a pristine 2007 Porsche 911 Turbo.

2012

150-Point Inspection Standard

We introduce our industry-leading 150-point inspection process, setting a new benchmark for pre-owned luxury vehicle certification. This becomes our signature quality guarantee.

2016

Global Sourcing Network

Establishment of our worldwide sourcing network, allowing us to locate and acquire rare specifications from Europe, Asia, and the Middle East for discerning collectors.

2020

Luxury Retail Award

Recognition as "Luxury Automotive Retailer of the Year" by the International Automotive Association, cementing our position as an industry leader in premium vehicle sales.

2024

The Future of Luxury

Launch of our Virtual Concierge Service and expansion into electric hypercar curation, positioning AutoElite at the forefront of automotive innovation for the next generation.

Our Methodology

The AutoElite Process

A meticulously crafted journey from selection to delivery.

01

Global Sourcing

Our network scouts worldwide for vehicles meeting our exacting standards of quality, history, and specification.

02
